L’utilisateur s’engage à fournir des coordonnées exactes et à ne pas transmettre de données médicales sensibles dans le formulaire public.

Terms of use

1. Purpose

The website allows visitors to review services and send an organisation request. Sending the form does not confirm an appointment or guarantee care.

2. Confirmation

Availability, service scope, possible additional costs and the time slot are confirmed by the team before the service. Do not pay anything before confirmation.
